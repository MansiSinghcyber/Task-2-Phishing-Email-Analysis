# Task 2 - Phishing Email Analysis

## Email Sample

From: security@bankofamerica-alert.com

Subject: Urgent: Account Verification Required

Body:
Your account will be locked in 24 hours.
Verify now at:
https://secure-login-verify.com

---

## Phishing Indicators

### 1. Sender Email Spoofing

The sender claims to be Bank of America.

Actual sender:

security@bankofamerica-alert.com

This is NOT the official domain.

Official domain:
bankofamerica.com

Therefore this is likely spoofed.

---

### 2. Urgent Language

The email says:

"Your account will be locked in 24 hours."

This creates panic and forces the victim to click quickly.

---

### 3. Suspicious Link

The link is:

https://secure-login-verify.com

This is not owned by Bank of America.

The attacker is trying to steal login credentials.

---

### 4. Social Engineering

The attacker creates fear and urgency.

Victim is tricked into believing immediate action is required.

---

### 5. Credential Harvesting

Purpose:
Steal username and password.

---

### 6. Brand Impersonation

The attacker pretends to be Bank of America.

This increases trust.

---

## Conclusion

The email contains multiple phishing indicators:

- Spoofed sender
- Fake domain
- Urgent language
- Credential harvesting attempt
- Suspicious URL
- Social engineering

Hence this email is classified as a phishing email.
